Label Redesign · Round 2 · For Review

Five fronts, two backs

Round 2 per your notes: the DCM logo is featured prominently on every front, and the back is stripped down. All at exact 2.8″ × 0.8″ proportions, 500 DPI, and all inside the same print-safe spec as Round 1 (<25% ink, gradients confined to small solids, QR untouched black-on-white).

Front 1 — Emblem Block

Boldest logo presence~24% ink coverage
  • The white logo owns a full-height purple block — unmistakably DCM at slab distance, the strongest brand read of the five.
  • Largest solid of any variant; still prints safely (one flat block, no wide gradient), but it uses the most ink of this set.

Front 2 — Certificate Seal

Most premium / traditional~12% ink coverage
  • Double gold rule frame + circular grade seal reads like an authentication certificate — the most “heritage” of the set.
  • The round seal is a different grade shape than every competitor label (PSA/BGS/CGC all use squares/bars).

Front 3 — Top Banner

Brand + name lockup~28% ink coverage
  • Logo + “DCM GRADING” wordmark + serial all live in the banner, so the ivory area stays purely about the card.
  • The grade chip breaks through the band — the one deliberate flourish, and it makes the grade the tallest element on the label.

Front 4 — Swiss

Most minimal · least ink~8% ink coverage
  • The grade IS the design: a 300px numeral, rotated MINT, and the navy logo at full size on pure white.
  • Cheapest to print and the cleanest at small scale; the risk is it reads more “modern gallery” than “heritage.”

Front 5 — Gold Marque

Balanced middle ground~11% ink coverage
  • Gold corner ticks frame the label like a mat board; the navy logo sits at near-full block size without needing a colored band.
  • The gold keyline inside the grade chip ties the chip to the corner ticks — smallest set of moves, most cohesive result.

Back A — Minimal

Proposed default back~10% ink · QR scannable at 0.5″
  • Everything Round 1 had minus the four sub-scores and emblem chips: QR, grade, serial, verify URL, logo. Sub-grades stay on the card detail page the QR opens.

Back B — with emblems

When the collector has emblems~11% ink coverage
  • Emblems collapse from Round 1’s outlined chips to one quiet inline row (max 2, per your rule) — present but no longer competing with the QR.
  • Back A is what ships when the collector has no emblems; Back B is the same layout with the one extra line.

My read

  • Front 5 (Gold Marque) is the strongest all-rounder: prominent logo, heritage cues, low ink, and it keeps the left band free for the card-color / pattern customizations from Round 1.
  • Front 1 (Emblem Block) is the pick if brand visibility in a display case is the top priority.
  • Back A as the default, auto-upgrading to Back B when the account has emblems.
