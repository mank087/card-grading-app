# Cards that would move from 9 to 10 under the new confidence rule

Every card below was graded **9**, and all three independent evaluations scored it **10**. The only thing holding it at 9 was the image-confidence letter "C". None is in a sleeve or holder, none has a corner out of frame, and the magnified inspection covered every region of both faces.

**The question for each one: looking at the photos, would you call this card a 10?** If most are, the rule is right. If several are not, it is wrong and we stop.

Window: cards graded since Sept 18, when inspection coverage started being recorded. 30 of 537 cards move, taking the 10-rate from 22.2% to 27.7%. The grader's own notes put the healthy baseline near 24.5%, so this is a real increase and worth your judgement.
