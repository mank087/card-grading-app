# My look at the 34 cards the confidence change would have moved from 9 to 10

For each card I looked at the front and back overviews and all eight corners cropped at full resolution. I examined 28 closely.

| Verdict | Cards |
| --- | --- |
| Looks like a 10 | 5 |
| Borderline | 4 |
| Cannot support a 10 | 18 |
| Not examined | 7 |

**My opinion: the confidence letter was mostly right, and the change should not ship.** About one card in six was photographed well enough to support a 10. The rest were dim, soft, blurred or hand-held, and a corner ding or edge whitening would simply not be visible in them. A caveat on my own judgement: I can see sharpness, lighting and obvious flaws, but telling a true 9 from a 10 off a phone photo is exactly the hard problem, so on the five I marked as 10s I am saying "nothing visible argues against it", not certifying them.

## Looks like a 10

- [Pat Surtain II](https://dcmgrading.com/sports/25d7070e-f36d-4b7a-b0ba-4c03108e7e80) · Sports  
  Sharp, evenly lit, fills 94% of the frame. Corners crisp front and back. The C looks unjustified.
- [Pikachu ex](https://dcmgrading.com/pokemon/5a6a60ae-7db0-4fc4-b709-05d6a1978c16) · Pokemon  
  Good light and focus. Corners clean on both faces. Holo glare is probably what drew the C.
- [Superman](https://dcmgrading.com/other/7743571d-2fd8-419e-a8b5-5d1f7a2818d3) · Other  
  Sharpest photo in the set, on a dark mat. Corners clean; one tiny white fleck on the front lower edge is most likely dust.
- [Derek Jeter](https://dcmgrading.com/sports/bee50bf0-01d3-4a21-a0e9-78548276b17d) · Sports  
  Sharp on a dark mat. White back corners are clean. Chrome glare hides some of the front surface.
- [Eevee](https://dcmgrading.com/pokemon/ef983992-512d-4aee-9e7f-5f1d8f0685d9) · Pokemon  
  Front and back both reasonably sharp, corners clean. Hand-held, with holo glare streaks.

## Cannot support a 10

- [Rocket's Hideout](https://dcmgrading.com/pokemon/0a970b19-ebd9-4776-b9c7-9ea06f06f746) · Pokemon  
  Dark room on a dark blanket, heavy noise. Possible whitening on a back corner. A 2000-era card.
- [Philippe](https://dcmgrading.com/pokemon/0f3721ea-ef11-4ffb-82cc-bd3f65079d29) · Pokemon  
  Low resolution (card only about 850 px wide) and motion blur.
- [Charizard V](https://dcmgrading.com/pokemon/1413d6f5-36c1-4ece-a916-f9e28b3c4b1b) · Pokemon  
  Dark and grainy; the card fills about a third of the frame; back corners too soft to read.
- [Davante Adams](https://dcmgrading.com/sports/2c9d3167-9d81-4b2e-b78a-4eeafeefde6c) · Sports  
  Die-cut card, so the measured corners are not the card's real corners. Moderately soft.
- [Drake Baldwin](https://dcmgrading.com/sports/452b6be1-9431-433a-9f2f-10d52742aac5) · Sports
